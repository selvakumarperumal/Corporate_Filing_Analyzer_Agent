Item 1A. Risk Factors
1. Credit and Interest Rate Risk: Adverse fluctuations in macroeconomic interest rates could increase default rates in commercial loan portfolios and compress net interest margins.
2. Cybersecurity and Technology Disruption: Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, ransomware, or unauthorized system access could compromise customer data and result in substantial financial penalties and reputational harm (Severity: High).
3. Regulatory and Capital Adequacy Risks: Stricter capital reserve mandates under Basel III/IV reforms could limit dividend distributions and capital deployment strategies.
